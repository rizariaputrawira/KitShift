# Match datasets

1. Bind both dataset snapshots and stable source-record locators, entity/observation meanings, admissible identifiers and attributes, allowed normalizations, expected relationship (`one_to_one`, `one_to_many`, `many_to_one`, or explicitly justified many-to-many), compute/output bounds and matching-error consequences. Link records across datasets, not transitive clustering or within-file deduplication. Establish the correspondence ledger before an enriched join. If relationship or join semantics are unresolved, return labeled candidates/issues and withhold a trusted combined dataset.
2. For raw or insufficiently profiled inputs, discover and read the enabled [kitshift-data-preparation owner](../kitshift-data-preparation/SKILL.md); prepared inputs may enter directly after key/lineage evidence is checked. Before exact or normalized matching, read [exact matching](references/exact-matching.md). Check coverage, multiplicities, composite-key completeness, namespace/time scope and null policy. Missing key components cannot match by ordinary equality; literal `NA` remains a value when the source contract says so. Blank normalized strings are not evidence.
3. Use trusted raw identifiers first, then justified normalized identifiers. A shared name alone is not a trusted unique identifier. Accept deterministic correspondences only when key semantics, uniqueness/cardinality and conflicting-attribute rules permit. Detect normalization collisions, reused targets and conflicting keys before acceptance. Never hide violations with `DISTINCT`, `drop_duplicates`, aggregation or an unchecked many-to-many merge. A later fuzzy stage cannot silently override a trusted match or reassign its target under a one-to-one contract.
4. When approximate attributes are needed, read [entity resolution](references/entity-resolution.md). Separate candidate generation/blocking, scoring and decisions; estimate volume before scoring, use explicit bounded rules and disclose missed-match risk. A truncated search must label its coverage and cannot certify absent records as definitive nonmatches. Preserve candidate evidence, disagreements, missingness, scorer/preprocessing/scale, alternatives, ties and conflicts. Opaque tie ordering is not evidence. Do not fuzzy-match identifier numbers or assume unsupported transliteration/name equivalence.
5. Approximate correspondences default to review-required. Automatically accept only when the assignment explicitly authorizes a defined rule supported by reviewed representative labeled evidence and its validation/error tradeoff. Never invent a cutoff or probability interpretation. Record review decisions separately for reproducible authorized reruns. Unresolved ties, contradictory reliable attributes and relationship violations remain unaccepted. Candidate coverage and pair accuracy differ; synthetic examples or unlabeled pairs do not establish production precision/recall.
6. Before combining columns, read [reconciliation](references/reconciliation.md). Materialize accepted correspondences only, under the declared join type/grain. Check actual multiplicities; retain source/side identity of overlapping columns rather than silently coalescing conflicts. Explain expansion, unmatched populations and control totals. A cardinality failure makes partial evidence provisional and blocks a trusted combined result.
