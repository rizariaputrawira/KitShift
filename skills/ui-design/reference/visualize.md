# Visualize: Direction Comps & Asset Production

Use for explicitly requested visual comps or a selected comp-led workflow when image generation and its effects are authorized. Follow [Workflow and authority](../SKILL.md#workflow-and-authority). Ordinary UI work needs neither image generation nor PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, phase state, scripts or services. Reuse the user's brief, actual identity evidence and any settled direction; do not reopen choices already made. Mode references inform composition but cannot override the brief. A prior displayed and genuinely selected comp can discharge the same direction choice without regenerating it.

A probe tests composition, narrative, hierarchy, density, focal moment, signature use and image requirements, not a second identity workshop. Preserve the established palette, typography, materials, imagery and motion where the current brief does not change them. DESIGN.md is evidence of that identity when present, not a prerequisite or authority above the user.

## Generate compositional options

For a selected persistent helper build, use its actual phase protocol: `impeccable build-phase start --direction <seed key> --kind <...>` before writing comps under `.impeccable/mocks/`. That helper may require an open `comps` phase. Native image tools and ordinary comp exploration are not bound to helper state; save only the requested artifacts at authorized paths.

Render the number of alternatives the user requested. Three is a useful default for unresolved composition, not a mandatory quota or reason to redo a settled choice. Match the actual viewport: portrait device dimensions for native/mobile-first surfaces, desktop landscape when appropriate. Work directly or use justified native delegation with a self-contained brief; the controller must inspect the returned images. Open assets with the available image tool's supported path form. Base prompts on real content and settled concepts. For an incumbent identity, use supplied or permitted screenshots as references, explaining which palette/type/component qualities transfer and which page-specific content/layout must not leak. Vary meaningful structural uncertainty rather than making a tidier copy of the first comp.

- A comp is a designed surface, not a picture of the subject. Lead the prompt with the surface's own structure: the regions this design has, named in order with their scale relationships; a page with no navigation says so instead of inventing one, and an unconventional surface states its unconventional skeleton. A prompt that leads with atmosphere gets a vignette back: the model paints the fish market instead of the fish market's website. How far the world reaches into the page, and what it may carry, is the mode's call, in its COMPS rules. Render the page itself edge to edge: no browser window, address bar, device or desk around it, so prompt it as the page, never as a screenshot of a browser. Self-check every render: if it could hang as a poster, or reads as a photograph with some text on it, it is not a comp; regenerate with the dealt composition's regions named and its parts working in place.
- The inverse is also a failure: a surface with none of its subject in it. The subject appears as the content the regions hold; the world dresses the frame and never displaces what the frame shows. The deletion usually rides in on the prompt's exclusion list, so exclusions bind invented claims, and a medium ban belongs to the committed imagery stance, never to caution. Before accepting a render, point at the subject; a render that depicts everything about the world and nothing of the subject fails however faithful its atmosphere. Regenerate with the subject's content named region by region.
- Judge a comp as the shipped screen: the visitor's job must be readable from the image alone. Name the surface's mode from the render with no caption; a render whose mode cannot be read back is art direction without a surface. Regenerate with the visitor's job as the prompt's spine.
- Commitment is depth, not performance spread across regions. The world enters through one dominant move plus the material, type, and spacing that support it; the remaining regions hold still in that material so the move can be read. A region that simply does its job in the world's grammar carries the direction further than a region performing the concept. The check cuts competition, never content: a quieted region keeps its information and stops performing, and the page still carries its content (a way to navigate, the offer, its supporting copy, an action, a glimpse of what follows) at real reading size (body text near 16px on a 1440px-wide page). That is a list of content, not a layout: where each piece sits is the composition's call. A second element competing with the named focal moment at the same scale means the comp is shouting; with no named focal moment, several regions performing the concept at once is the same shout. Regenerate keeping the strongest move and quieting the rest. Busy is louder, not bolder.
- When the user shortlisted multiple concepts, spread the three across them.
- When one direction is committed, vary the structural uncertainty an image can resolve: topology, sequence, density, hierarchy, focal composition, or interaction framing.
- Show enough beyond the opening moment to prove the concept can govern the whole surface.
- Do not generate a palette artifact, ask new atmosphere questions, introduce a different type voice, or invent a new motif. If the committed world cannot support the concept, return to the concept shortlist rather than changing the world.

Each comp is a direction test, not a screenshot specification. Core UI text, responsive behavior, accessibility, semantics, and interaction states remain implementation responsibilities.

## One approval point

Show the alternatives through a permitted image-capable native interface. A helper decision page (`impeccable serve-question`) is optional only when its writes/server/browser effects are covered. Ask about concrete carry-forward choices only when unresolved. Use real user responses or an explicit delegation of choice; a simulated role is not the user.

If the user requested an approval checkpoint, do not implement past it without actual approval or explicit delegation. If choice was already delegated, decide using the brief and evidence and disclose the decision without inventing an approval receipt. A failed question tool, unavailable browser, timeout or silence never delegates authority.

For an authorized persistent helper workflow, record the actual selected comp path in its surface brief and a truthful prompt sidecar (`a.png.json` for `a.png`). Mark `"approved": true` only for actual user approval; document a user-delegated selection distinctly instead of inventing a human receipt. If a selected helper requires an approval it has not received, report that checkpoint as pending rather than manufacturing state to advance it. For ordinary comps, return images and selection in the requested channel without initializing state or writing receipts.

## After approval: the comp becomes a spec

The approved comp is a north star for translation into semantic, responsive, accessible code, never a license to recompose: keeping the palette and mood while redrawing the topology is a second art direction. Do not rasterize core UI text or controls. Do not substitute a different visual driver after approval without asking.

Measure the actual comp before translation. In a selected helper build, `impeccable comp-spec` can produce region boxes and palette readings; otherwise native image inspection and a direct region description are valid. Respect the requested medium and selected design: detailed figures, shaded objects and material textures often require image assets, while text, controls, responsive chrome and countable diagrams belong in semantic code. Do not silently delete an approved visual driver because another medium is easier. The user's brief and genuine acceptance govern medium choices; detector scores do not impose a universal raster ban or mandate. Generated imagery must not masquerade as real evidence, product photography, testimonials or factual claims.

## Plates and provenance

Produce needed assets directly or through justified native delegation using [asset-producer.md](degraded/asset-producer.md) as a prompt reference, not a registered role or model guarantee. In a selected helper build, `impeccable comp-spec --crop <id>` and `--plate-prompt <id>` can supply a crop/prompt for a native image tool or an authorized `impeccable generate-image --ref <crop.png> --prompt-file <prompt.txt> --out <plate.png> --size <WxH> --quality high` call. Transparent cutouts need real alpha; inspect on light and dark grounds. Preserve source/prompt provenance accurately. `impeccable embed-prompt` is an optional authorized metadata write, not compulsory for every native image. Record stock/pre-existing origin instead of inventing a generation prompt, and delete abandoned assets only when created by this task or explicitly in scope.

Use available permitted image converters when needed. Missing tooling is not authority to install it or run context/bootstrap probes, and this reference sets no arbitrary probe or iteration ceiling.
