## Local correction basis

[RFC 2606](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2606) identifies reserved testing/documentation domains. The address samples now use those namespaces; this reduces identity collisions, not network access or delivery authority. Multi-label `.test` samples do not prove public-suffix parsing. [MDN's title-attribute guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Global_attributes/title) documents keyboard, touch, and assistive-technology limitations; accessible full-value recovery cannot be established by a title tooltip alone.

The local pass retains read-only proposal, authorized harness, and requested-fix branches. It does not establish real identities, actual customer constraints, automatic backend-limit changes, a production exclusion mechanism, or an OS/network sandbox. Production exclusion and real request effects require project-specific evidence when a harness is authorized.
