# Existing-motion audit checklist

For a surface audit, read the seven numbered dimensions in [review standards](review-standards.md#review-dimensions). That is the single owner of the criteria: purpose/frequency, timing/tokens, origin/physicality, interruption, performance, accessibility, and cohesion.

Apply each dimension to the requested surface, recording actual file/line evidence and the consumer consequence, or marking it checked with no finding, absent, or out of scope. Separate source risks from observed browser/device behavior; compatible intentional tradeoffs are not defects. A clean audit is valid.

The [existing-motion procedure](existing-motion.md) owns audit reporting, planning, execution, and their permission boundaries. This checklist supplies criteria, not a second lifecycle or authorization to write. Diff-specific output and verdict rules belong only to an explicitly requested [diff review](diff-review.md).
