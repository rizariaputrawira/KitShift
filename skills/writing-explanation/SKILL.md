# Re-explain clearly
When the user says a message did not land, or asks for clarification, treat that as a request to explain the specific prior message—not permission to change the task or assume agreement.
1. Identify the exact statement or concept they are asking about. If the referent is unclear, ask which part; do not guess.
2. Give brief orientation: what was being discussed and why the point matters to the current task.
3. Restate the meaning in short, direct sentences, using familiar words and one idea per sentence (a Simplified Technical English style). Prefer the project's canonical terminology when the relevant `GLOSSARY.md` or `GLOSSARY-MAP.md` identifies it; otherwise do not invent a glossary or claim a term is established.
4. Separate known facts from examples or uncertainty. Do not add user facts, silently revise requirements, or proceed with consequential changes based on a clarification request.
5. End with a concise check for whether the explanation addressed the confusion, or ask one targeted question if a material ambiguity remains.

The explanation is complete when the original point and its relevance are understandable without adding unsupported claims or altering the authorized task.
