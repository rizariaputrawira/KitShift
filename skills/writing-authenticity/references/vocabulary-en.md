# English vocabulary watchlist

Use within [the writing review procedure](../SKILL.md#procedure) for English human-facing text. These are editorial prompts, not banned tokens, authorship evidence or automatic replacement rules. The parent procedure owns permissions and preservation requirements.

## Words and phrases to inspect

| Wording | Inspect for | Rewrite when the concern applies | Keep when |
|---|---|---|---|
| utilize, leverage, harness | An ordinary action hidden by business language | State the action: use, read, run, reuse, or another accurate verb. | It names a technical operation or literal object, such as financial leverage or a test harness. |
| facilitate, enable, empower | An unspecified actor, mechanism or benefit | Identify who can do what and how, using supplied facts. | The distinction between permitting an action and performing it matters. |
| optimize, streamline, enhance | An improvement with no defined dimension | Name the change: fewer steps, lower latency, clearer labels, or the actual supported effect. | It denotes a defined optimization objective or an accurately scoped improvement. |
| robust, seamless, intuitive | An unsupported quality claim | State the supported behavior or remove the praise; report an evidence gap rather than invent proof. | The term has a defined domain meaning or the text supplies relevant evidence. |
| innovative, revolutionary, groundbreaking | Novelty asserted without a comparison | Describe what changed; retain a novelty claim only with a supported basis. | A sourced comparison establishes the claimed novelty. |
| crucial, pivotal, essential | Importance asserted instead of explained | Identify the dependency or consequence, or delete the intensifier. | Necessity or a decisive role is actually established. |
| significant, significantly | Statistical and ordinary meanings blurred | State the magnitude or explain the practical consequence when available. | It accurately describes a reported statistical result; preserve its qualification and evidence. |
| landscape, ecosystem, paradigm | A vague metaphor obscuring scope | Name the actual field, participants, dependencies or model. | It is literal or established technical terminology, such as an ecological ecosystem or programming paradigm. |
| delve into, explore, unpack | An announcement replacing substantive content | Begin with the finding or identify the concrete investigative action. | Exploration itself is the authorized activity or the word has a literal meaning. |
| comprehensive, holistic | Coverage promised without boundaries | Specify the covered scope and known exclusions. | The scope is defined and the coverage claim is justified. |
| it is important to note that, it is worth mentioning that | A preface delaying the point | Start with the fact; preserve any warning or qualification that follows. | The act of noting or recording is itself relevant to the task. |
| furthermore, moreover, additionally | Repeated transitions or additions without a clear relationship | Remove redundant framing or make the relationship explicit. | The connector helps the reader follow a real addition. |
| in conclusion, overall, in summary | An unnecessary recap | End with the result or required next action. | The format calls for a conclusion or a summary helps the audience. |
| experts say, studies show, research suggests | Attribution that cannot be checked | Identify the supplied source; if absent, flag the unsupported claim. | The source is already unambiguous in the surrounding text. |
| may potentially, could possibly | Duplicated uncertainty | Retain the qualifier that matches the evidence. | Distinct qualifications really convey different uncertainties. |

## Worked decisions

Synthetic editorial examples; not reports of observed product behavior:

- **Simplify without changing the action:** “Utilize the export command to save the report.” → “Use the export command to save the report.”
- **Make no unsupported upgrade:** “The interface is seamless.” With no supporting details, an audit flags the unsupported quality claim; it does not invent a click count or rewrite it as “The interface is easy to use.”
- **Preserve technical meaning:** “The estimate was not statistically significant.” → unchanged. Replacing *significant* with *important* would change the claim.
- **Keep a useful connector:** “The export includes archived records. Additionally, it includes their attachments.” *Additionally* is not an error by itself; change only if the surrounding passage makes the transition repetitive or unnecessary.
