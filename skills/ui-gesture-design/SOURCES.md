## Primary references and local interpretation

- [Designing Fluid Interfaces — WWDC18](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2018/803/): historical gesture/motion context, not a guarantee of current Apple implementation or web/native parity.
- [Designing Audio-Haptic Experiences — WWDC19](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2019/810/): named audio/haptic design context; browser support and delivery have separate constraints.
- [The details of UI typography — WWDC20](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10175/): platform type context, not a requirement to replace product fonts or tokens.
- [Principles of great design — WWDC26](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/250/): the primary page lists the eight retained principles as chapters.
- [Motion transitions](https://motion.dev/docs/react-transitions): documented physics-based versus duration/bounce spring and velocity behavior.
- [MDN will-change](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/will-change) and [Vibration API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Vibration_API): rendering-hint costs and capability-dependent vibration.

Former quotation-style passages are now local paraphrases, not asserted verbatim transcripts. The retained ratio/response values and projection coefficient are illustrative adaptation material, not independently verified current Apple defaults.
