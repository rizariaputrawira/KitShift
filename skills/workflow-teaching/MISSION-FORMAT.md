# Mission format

`MISSION.md` captures the concrete reason the user is learning and guides future lessons.

```md
# Mission: <Topic>

## Why
<One to three sentences naming the real-world outcome.>

## Success looks like
- <Observable capability>

## Constraints
- <Time, budget, prior commitments or preferences>

## Out of scope
- <Explicitly excluded adjacent topics>
```

Keep it short and specific. Ask before revising an established mission; a new or unclear mission is a reason to interview, not guess.
