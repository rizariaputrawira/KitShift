# Glossary format

`GLOSSARY.md` records terminology the learner can already use; it is not a teaching dictionary.

```md
# <Topic> Glossary

<One or two sentences describing its scope.>

## Terms

**<Term>**: <One or two sentence definition.>
_Avoid_: <Confusable aliases, when useful.>
```

Add terms after demonstrated understanding, choose a preferred term, keep definitions concise, use established glossary terms consistently, and make domain-specific ambiguity explicit. Revise stale definitions as understanding deepens.
